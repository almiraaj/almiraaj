✅ Unmarried Certificate Apostille – Frequently Asked Questions

An Unmarried Certificate, also known as a Single Status Certificate or Certificat de célibat, is a legal document confirming that a person is unmarried. Apostille is the Hague Convention process that makes this certificate valid internationally without further embassy attestation. It is required for individuals planning to marry abroad, register civil partnerships, or apply for visas and immigration where proof of single status is mandatory.

Commonly Apostilled documents include:
  • 🧾 Unmarried / Single Status Certificate
  • 🎓 Educational Certificates (Degree, Diploma, Mark Sheets) – Learn more
  • 🔒 Police Clearance Certificates (PCC)
  • 📜 Other Personal Certificates (Birth, Marriage, Divorce, Death)

  1. ✅ Notary Attestation – Initial verification of the certificate
  2. ✅ State Home Department Authentication – State-level approval
  3. ✅ MEA Apostille – Ministry of External Affairs issues the Apostille sticker/seal
Once Apostilled, your Unmarried Certificate is directly valid in all Hague Convention countries without further embassy legalization.

Typically 5–7 working days depending on the issuing authority and verification process. Urgent services may be available for faster processing if required for immediate marriage registration abroad.
