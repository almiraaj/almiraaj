Unmarried Certificate Apostille FAQs

An Unmarried Certificate, also known as a Single Status Certificate or Bachelorhood Certificate, confirms that a person is legally unmarried. Apostille authentication makes the document valid for marriage registration, spouse visa applications, and legal procedures in Hague Convention countries.

Commonly required documents include an Unmarried Certificate Apostille, Birth Certificate Apostille, Educational Certificate Apostille, Police Clearance Certificate (PCC), Passport Copy, and other supporting personal documents requested by the destination country.

The process generally includes Notary Attestation, State Authentication or SDM Verification, followed by Apostille certification from the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA). Once completed, the document becomes valid in Hague Convention member countries. For a smooth and hassle-free process, many applicants choose AL-MIRAAJ International for professional assistance with document verification, authentication, MEA Apostille processing, and status updates.

Processing usually takes approximately 5 to 7 working days depending on the issuing authority, verification requirements, and Ministry of External Affairs processing timelines.
