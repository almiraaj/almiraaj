COC - Chamber of Commerce Attestation

What is Chamber of Commerce Attestation?

Chamber of Commerce Attestation, often referred to as COC attestation, involves the certification or authentication of eligible commercial documents by a Chamber of Commerce or other authorized organization, depending on the document and destination country requirements.

Commercial documents may need certification or attestation before they can be submitted to foreign authorities, business partners, customs authorities, embassies, consulates, banks, or other organizations involved in an international transaction.

The exact procedure depends on the document type, issuing authority, destination country, and intended purpose. Some documents may require additional legalization or embassy attestation after Chamber of Commerce processing.

Important: COC requirements vary according to the destination country and commercial transaction. AL-MIRAAJ can help identify the applicable document and legalization requirements before submission.

Why is COC Attestation Required?

Commercial documents may require authentication when they are being used for international trade, business transactions, exports, imports, or overseas corporate activities.

International Trade

Supports documentation used for export and import transactions and international commercial activities.

Business Transactions

Helps businesses prepare commercial documentation for overseas buyers, suppliers, partners, and authorities.

Further Legalization

Where required, Chamber certification may form part of a wider legalization or embassy-attestation process.

Documents for COC Chamber of Commerce Attestation

Depending on the destination country and transaction, businesses may require certification or attestation for different types of commercial documents.

Commercial Invoice

Commercial invoices used in international sales and export transactions.

Certificate of Origin

Documents identifying the origin of goods for international trade, subject to applicable issuing requirements.

Packing List

Export packing lists and related commercial shipping documentation.

Shipping Documents

Selected shipping and export documents may require certification or attestation according to transaction requirements.

Business Agreements

Commercial contracts, agreements, declarations, and related corporate documents.

Power of Attorney

Business powers of attorney and authorization documents where certification is required.

Note: The documents accepted for Chamber certification or attestation can differ by Chamber, destination country, and purpose. Please confirm the applicable requirements before submitting original documents.

COC Attestation vs Apostille vs Embassy Attestation

Service Main Purpose Typical Use
Chamber of Commerce Attestation Certification or authentication of eligible commercial documents. International trade and business documentation.
MEA Apostille Authentication under the Hague Apostille framework. Eligible documents intended for countries recognizing Apostilles.
Embassy Attestation Legalization by the relevant foreign embassy or consular authority. Countries or transactions requiring embassy/consular legalization.

These services are not interchangeable. The correct route depends on the document, destination country, and purpose of use.
