✅ MEA Apostille in Chennai – Frequently Asked Questions

MEA Apostille is an authentication process issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. It makes Indian documents legally acceptable in Hague Convention member countries for employment, higher education, immigration, family visas and business purposes.

AL-MIRAAJ INTERNATIONAL provides professional Apostille services for educational, personal and commercial documents. We assist with document verification, authentication and MEA Apostille processing while ensuring secure handling, timely updates and doorstep document collection in selected serviceable locations.

Common Apostille documents include Degree Certificates, Diplomas, Mark Sheets, Birth Certificates, Marriage Certificates, Police Clearance Certificates (PCC), Power of Attorney, Affidavits, Legal Heir Certificates and Commercial Documents.

The Apostille process generally involves document verification, State Authentication or HRD approval where applicable, followed by Apostille authentication from the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India.

Apostille documents are accepted in Hague Convention member countries including the USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Australia and many other countries.

Processing time depends on document type and authentication requirements. Most Apostille applications are completed within a few working days after submission of the required documents.
