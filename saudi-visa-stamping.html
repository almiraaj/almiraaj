Understanding QVP & SVP for Saudi Employment Visa

Saudi Arabia verifies professional qualifications and trade skills to ensure workers entering the Kingdom meet industry standards and regulatory requirements.

🎓

QVP

Qualification Verification Program

Verification of educational qualifications and professional certifications for skilled professionals.

🛠️

SVP

Skill Verification Program

Practical and theoretical skill assessment for workers employed in trade-based professions.

QVP – Qualification Verification Program

Purpose: Verifies educational degrees and professional certifications.

Who Needs It: Engineers, Doctors, Accountants, Teachers and other professionals whose occupations require formal qualifications.

Process:

  • Submit educational certificates through the QVP portal.
  • Certificates are verified for authenticity.
  • Qualification is matched against the proposed job role.
  • Verification clearance is issued for visa processing.
Example: An Engineer applying for a Saudi Employment Visa may be required to complete QVP verification before visa stamping.
Visit QVP Portal
SVP – Skill Verification Program

Purpose: Assesses practical trade skills and technical competency.

Who Needs It: Electricians, Plumbers, Mechanics, Drivers, Welders, Technicians and other trade-based workers.

Process:

  • Attend theory and practical assessments.
  • Tests are conducted at approved examination centres.
  • Skills are evaluated according to Saudi standards.
  • Successful candidates receive verification clearance.
Example: A Plumber applying for a Saudi Employment Visa may need to pass the SVP assessment before visa stamping.
Visit SVP Portal
