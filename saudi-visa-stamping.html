🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia Visa Stamping & Processing Services

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia remains one of the most popular destinations for employment, tourism, Umrah pilgrimage, business expansion and family visits. Whether you are applying for a Saudi Tourist Visa, Commercial Visit Visa, Employment Visa, Resident Visa, Family Visit Visa or Umrah Visa, proper document verification and visa stamping are essential. Depending on the visa category, applicants may require invitation approval, employer authorization (Wakala), passport processing, certificate legalization and embassy submission. AL-MIRAAJ INTERNATIONAL assists clients throughout Chennai, Tamil Nadu and India with Saudi visa documentation, certificate legalization, apostille and embassy attestation services.

📌 Passport copy, photograph and visa application required.

Saudi Commercial Visit Visa

🏢 For business meetings, negotiations, exhibitions and trade visits.
📄 Requires Saudi company invitation approval.
✅ Suitable for business owners, executives and representatives.

Umrah Visa Services

🕋 Travel for Umrah pilgrimage with professional assistance.

✅ Individual and family Saudi Umrah Visa Processing Services with complete guidance for pilgrims travelling from India.
✈️ Assistance for Umrah visa, flight booking, hotel reservation and Umrah travel packages.

Saudi Employment Visa

💼 Requires a valid Saudi job offer and employer sponsorship.

📑 Wakala / MOFA Authorization is generally required before Saudi visa stamping.

✅ Professionals falling under SVP (Skill Verification Program) categories may be required to complete professional skill verification before visa processing.

✅ Professionals under QVP (Qiwa Verification Program) categories may be required to complete qualification and professional credential verification prior to employment visa issuance.

📋 Saudi Employment Visa Process:
✅ Employer Issues Visa & Wakala Authorization.
✅ SVP / QVP Verification (if applicable), GAMCA Medical Examination & Document Preparation.
✅ Apostille, Embassy Attestation, VFS Submission , Biometric Process, and Final Saudi Visa Stamping.

🎓 Educational certificates may require Apostille and Embassy Attestation before Saudi Embassy submission.

Saudi Resident Visa

🏠 For spouses, children and dependents joining family members residing in Saudi Arabia.
📄 Requires a valid Saudi Resident Visa authorization issued by the Saudi authorities and sponsorship from the resident family member.
🛂 Documents Required: Passport (current & old passport if available), passport-size photographs with white background, Saudi visa authorization copy, relationship proof documents and supporting records.
🏥 Adults may require a GAMCA Medical Examination prior to visa stamping. We can assist with GAMCA Medical Appointment Booking.
👶 For minor children, vaccination records may be required with Apostille and Arabic Translation wherever applicable.
📜 Marriage Certificates, Birth Certificates and other relationship proof documents may require Apostille and Embassy Attestation before submission.
📅 VFS Appointment Booking Assistance Available for biometric enrollment and document submission.
✅ Complete support for document review, apostille, attestation, translation, VFS appointment and Saudi Resident Visa stamping.



Saudi Family Visit Visa



👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For spouse, children, parents and eligible family members visiting relatives residing in Saudi Arabia.


📄 Requires a valid Saudi Family Visit Visa authorization issued through the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) and sponsorship from the family member residing in Saudi Arabia.


🛂 Documents Required: Valid Passport (minimum 6 months validity), recent white background photographs, Saudi Family Visit Visa authorization copy, sponsor's Iqama copy, sponsor's passport copy and relationship proof documents.


📜 Marriage Certificate for spouse, Birth Certificate for children and other relationship proof documents may require Apostille and Embassy Attestation wherever applicable before visa processing.


🌐 Arabic Translation assistance available for Marriage Certificates, Birth Certificates and supporting documents whenever required by the Saudi authorities.


❌ Medical Examination Not Required for Saudi Family Visit Visa applicants.


📅 VFS Appointment Booking Assistance Available for biometric enrollment and document submission if applicable.


✈️ Assistance available for travel insurance, flight booking and complete visa processing support.


✅ Complete support for document review, Apostille, Attestation, Translation, VFS Appointment and Saudi Family Visit Visa processing.
