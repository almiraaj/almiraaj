Wafid (GAMCA) Medical Appointment Services in Chennai

GAMCA Medical Appointment, now officially known as the Wafid Medical Program, is a mandatory medical screening requirement for individuals planning to work in GCC countries such as Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, and the United Arab Emirates (UAE). The medical examination is conducted through GCC-approved medical centers to ensure applicants meet the health requirements established by Gulf authorities before the issuance of an employment visa.

Individuals applying for employment visas, work permits, domestic worker visas, technician visas, engineer visas, driver visas, nurse visas, labor visas, and other job-related visas are generally required to complete the Wafid (formerly GAMCA) medical process before travelling to their destination country. The appointment must be booked through the official Wafid system, which automatically assigns the approved medical center for the medical examination.

Almiraaj International provides fast, reliable, and hassle-free assistance for Wafid (GAMCA) medical appointment booking. Our experienced team helps applicants verify passport details, complete the registration accurately, secure appointments instantly, and avoid common booking errors that can lead to delays or appointment rejections.

The official Wafid (formerly GAMCA) medical appointment booking fee is USD 10. Once the appointment is successfully generated, applicants can visit their designated GCC-approved medical center on the scheduled date to complete the required medical examination and laboratory tests.

Every year, thousands of professionals, skilled workers, and job seekers require a valid Wafid medical appointment before travelling to GCC countries for employment opportunities. Whether you are travelling to Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, or the UAE, our team provides quick appointment assistance, dependable guidance, and prompt support throughout the booking process.
