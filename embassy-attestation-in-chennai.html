✅ Embassy Attestation in Chennai – Frequently Asked Questions

Embassy Attestation is the process of verifying Indian documents by the respective country’s embassy to make them legally valid abroad. It is mandatory for employment visas, higher education, family residency, business setup, and medical treatment. Countries such as UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, Malaysia, China, Egypt, Philippines, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, Afghanistan, and Nepal require embassy attestation for non‑Apostille documents.

The documents commonly attested include: - Educational certificates (degree, diploma, transcripts) - Personal certificates (birth, marriage, medical, PCC) - Commercial documents (power of attorney, invoices, incorporation papers) These are required for employment visas, student visas, migration, business expansion, and family sponsorship.

The processing time depends on the country and type of document. - UAE, Qatar, Oman: 5–7 working days - Saudi Arabia, Kuwait: 7–10 working days - Malaysia, China, Egypt, Philippines, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand: 10–15 working days AL‑MIRAAJ INTERNATIONAL offers doorstep collection & delivery and provides fast‑track services for urgent requirements.
