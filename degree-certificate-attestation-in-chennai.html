✅ Degree Certificate Attestation – Frequently Asked Questions

A Degree Certificate Attestation is the process of verifying and authenticating your academic qualification for use abroad. It ensures your university degree is legally recognized by foreign governments, universities, and employers. This is required for students pursuing higher education abroad, professionals seeking employment opportunities overseas, and individuals applying for migration or work visas.

Commonly attested educational documents include:
  • 🎓 Degree Certificates
  • 📜 Diploma Certificates & Mark Sheets
  • 🏫 School Certificates (Secondary & Higher Secondary)
  • 📑 Professional Qualification Certificates

  1. ✅ Notary Attestation – Initial verification of the certificate
  2. ✅ University Verification – Authentication from the issuing institution
  3. ✅ State HRD Department – State-level educational authentication
  4. ✅ MEA Attestation – Ministry of External Affairs attestation
  5. ✅ Embassy Attestation – Final legalization by the destination country’s embassy
Once attested, your Degree Certificate is valid internationally for education, employment, and migration purposes.

Typically 7–10 working days depending on the issuing university, state HRD department, and embassy requirements. Expedited services may be available for urgent cases such as immediate overseas admission or job offers.
