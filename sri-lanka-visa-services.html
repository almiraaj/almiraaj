Sri Lanka is one of South Asia's most beautiful island destinations, famous for golden beaches, tropical landscapes, ancient heritage sites, tea plantations, wildlife parks, and welcoming hospitality. Whether you are planning a short holiday, family trip, honeymoon, business visit, or cultural exploration, Sri Lanka offers something for every traveller.

Popular Tourist Attractions in Sri Lanka

Colombo

The vibrant capital city offering shopping, nightlife, cultural landmarks, and seaside attractions.

Sigiriya

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress.

Kandy

Home to the famous Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and beautiful mountain scenery.

Nuwara Eliya

Known as Little England, famous for tea plantations and cool climate.

Ella

One of Sri Lanka's most scenic destinations featuring waterfalls and mountain views.

Bentota & Mirissa

Ideal destinations for beach holidays, whale watching, water sports, and relaxation.
