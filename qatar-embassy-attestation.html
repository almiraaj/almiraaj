Apostille vs Qatar Embassy Attestation

While Apostille certification is accepted in many Hague Convention countries, documents intended for official use in Qatar often require Embassy legalization. Therefore, educational certificates, personal certificates, and commercial documents generally undergo State Authentication, MEA Attestation, and Qatar Embassy Attestation before they can be officially used in Qatar.
