What is UAE Embassy Attestation?

UAE Embassy Attestation is the official process of validating Indian documents so they are legally accepted by UAE authorities. This attestation is mandatory for employment visas, higher education, business incorporation, medical treatment, family visas, and long-term residency permits in the United Arab Emirates.

Why Do You Need Attestation for UAE?

Attestation ensures your documents are recognized by the Government of UAE, employers, universities, and legal authorities. Without proper attestation, your applications for work, study, or residency may face delays or rejection.

Apostille vs UAE Embassy Attestation

Apostille is valid for Hague Convention countries. The UAE is not a Hague Convention member, so Apostille alone is not sufficient. A complete Embassy Attestation process is required, involving State, MEA, and UAE Embassy verification for educational, personal, and commercial documents.

  • Apostille: Not accepted in UAE
  • Embassy Attestation: Mandatory for all documents
