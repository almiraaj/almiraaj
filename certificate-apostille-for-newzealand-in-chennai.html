🔍 Process of Apostille for New Zealand

Learn how your documents get verified and authenticated for use in New Zealand.

📄 Step 1: Notarization

Documents must first be notarized by an authorized official in India to establish their authenticity before apostille processing begins.

🏛️ Step 2: Government Authentication

After notarization, authentication is required from relevant departments like the Home Department, State Government, or MEA (Ministry of External Affairs).

📬 Step 3: Apostille Stamp

Once authenticated, the MEA issues an Apostille stamp or sticker, confirming the document’s validity for use in New Zealand and other Hague Convention countries.

✅ Summary

This entire process ensures that your documents—whether personal, educational, or commercial—are legally accepted in New Zealand, easing pathways for study, work, migration, and business abroad.
