📑 Commercial Certificate Apostille Services – India

Commercial Certificate Apostille is the process of authenticating business and commercial documents for use in countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention. An apostille issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), India, confirms the authenticity of the document and eliminates the need for embassy legalization in participating countries.

Commercial Documents Eligible for Apostille: Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum of Association (MOA), Articles of Association (AOA), Board Resolution, Power of Attorney, Commercial Agreements, Export Invoices, Certificate of Origin, GST Documents, Trade Licenses, Company Registration Certificates, Partnership Deeds, and other business-related documents.

Commercial Apostille Process: The procedure generally involves Chamber of Commerce or relevant authority verification, State Authentication (where applicable), and final Apostille by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. The apostille certificate validates the document for official use in Hague Convention member countries.

Why Commercial Apostille is Required: Commercial document apostille is commonly required for international business expansion, company registration abroad, overseas branch setup, import-export activities, foreign investments, international tenders, banking formalities, contract execution, and cross-border commercial transactions.

Countries Accepting Apostille: Apostilled commercial documents are recognized in numerous Hague Convention countries including the Netherlands, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Belgium, Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Japan, and many other participating nations.

✅ Commercial Certificate Apostille in Chennai – Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Certificate Apostille is the process of authenticating business and trade documents issued in India so they are legally valid in Hague Convention countries. It is required for companies expanding overseas, entrepreneurs setting up branches, exporters/importers, and organizations entering into international contracts.

Commonly Apostilled commercial documents include:
  • 🏢 Memorandum of Association (MOA) & Articles of Association (AOA)
  • 📑 Certificate of Incorporation (COI)
  • 📜 Partnership Deeds & Board Resolutions
  • 🧾 Invoices, Agreements & Contracts
  • 📂 Export/Import Licenses & Trade Certificates

  1. ✅ Chamber of Commerce Attestation – Verification of business documents
  2. ✅ State Home Department / Relevant Authority Authentication
  3. ✅ MEA Apostille – Ministry of External Affairs issues the Apostille sticker/seal
Once Apostilled, your commercial documents are legally valid in all Hague Convention countries without further embassy legalization.

Typically 5–7 working days depending on the type of commercial document and issuing authority. Urgent Apostille services may be available for faster processing.
