✅ MEA Apostille & Attestation in Madurai – Frequently Asked Questions

MEA Apostille is the legalization process under the Hague Convention of 1961 that makes Indian documents valid in 120+ countries. Embassy Attestation is required for non‑Hague Convention countries. In Madurai, both services are vital for student visas, overseas employment, higher education, marriage registration, business expansion, and immigration.

Our Madurai branch processes:
  • Educational Certificates – Degree, Diploma, Mark Sheets
  • Personal Certificates – Birth, Marriage, Divorce, PCC
  • Commercial Certificates – Company Registration, Export Documents, Power of Attorney
All certificates are legalized to ensure global acceptance.

The Apostille process in Madurai involves:
  1. Notary Attestation – Initial verification
  2. State Home Department / HRD Authentication – State approval
  3. MEA Apostille – Apostille sticker/seal issued by Ministry of External Affairs
Once Apostilled, documents are valid in all Hague Convention countries.

The Attestation process in Madurai includes:
  1. Notary Attestation – Local verification
  2. State Home Department / HRD Authentication – State approval
  3. MEA Attestation – Ministry of External Affairs validation
  4. Embassy Attestation – Final legalization for non‑Hague Convention countries
This ensures acceptance in UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, and other non‑Hague nations.
