📌 Why Certificate Attestation is Required

Certificate attestation is mandatory for higher education, employment visas, family visas, business setup, and legal purposes abroad.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Attestation Process

  1. Notary Attestation – Initial verification of documents.
  2. State HRD/Home Department – Authentication by the issuing state.
  3. MEA Attestation – Ministry of External Affairs approval.
  4. Embassy Attestation – Legalization by the destination country’s embassy.
  5. MOFA Attestation – Final approval abroad.

📂 Types of Certificates We Attest

  • Educational: Degree, Diploma, School Certificates
  • Personal: Marriage, Birth, Divorce, Death, PCC, Affidavit
  • Commercial: Business contracts, invoices, Chamber of Commerce documents
