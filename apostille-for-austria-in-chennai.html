An Austria Apostille is an official authentication issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) that confirms the authenticity of Indian documents for use in Austria. Since Austria is a member of the Hague Apostille Convention, apostilled documents are generally accepted without further embassy legalization. Apostille certificates are commonly required for employment, higher studies, residence permits, family reunification, marriage registration, and business activities in Austria.
