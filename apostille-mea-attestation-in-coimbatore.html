Certificate Apostille is an official document authentication process issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India for use in Hague Convention member countries. Apostille confirms the authenticity of educational, personal and legal documents, allowing them to be accepted abroad for employment, higher education, immigration, family visa and business purposes.

We assist with Degree Certificate Apostille, Birth Certificate Apostille, Marriage Certificate Apostille, Divorce Certificate Apostille, Single Status Certificate Apostille, Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) Apostille, Power of Attorney Apostille, Legal Heir Certificate Apostille, Death Certificate Apostille, Affidavit Apostille, Medical Certificate Apostille and Experience Certificate Apostille.

Apostille documents are accepted in Hague Convention member countries such as Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Belgium, Australia, New Zealand and South Korea. Apostille eliminates the need for additional embassy legalization in participating countries.
